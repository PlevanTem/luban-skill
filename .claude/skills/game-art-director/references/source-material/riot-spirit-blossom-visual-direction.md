## Why this matters
This is a public case study of a Riot art-direction team taking a brand-new IP/season (Spirit Blossom) from thematic words → visual system → cross-platform guidelines. The pipeline is one of the cleanest publicly-documented examples of a "0→1 定调" process.

## Thematic foundation (the actual words used)
> "whimsical, spiritual, elegant, peaceful, abstract, and beguiling"

These 6 keywords were the **north star** for every subsequent decision. They came from the narrative team upstream, not from the art team inventing them.

> North star concept: "spirits interconnected in the spirit realm living in relative peace"

This is a phrase-level pillar, not a sentence-level brief.

## Shape language decision
> "geometry's intention is really up to what the viewer sees in it"

Choice: **geometry as primary visual system**. Rationale: abstraction supports the spiritual theme without locking interpretation.

References explored: East Asian floral / swirls / clouds.

## IP-grounding move (most transferable lesson)
Critical decision: **embed concept art from existing character skins into the design components themselves** — using "folklore patterns you see on Yasuo's shirt" as foundational elements.

This is the answer to "how do you make a brand-new visual season feel like it belongs to League of Legends":
- Pull patterns/textures from existing in-game character assets
- Use them as the literal ingredients of your new system
- The brand recognition becomes structural, not skin-deep

## Color & texture system
Watercolor aesthetic, drawn from:
- Tree bark texture
- Brush strokes
- Leaf veins

Goal: **dimensional surfaces without relying on flat color**. Implication: a 0→1 visual lead must specify *how depth is achieved* in the style, not just hue choices.

## Reusable framework (5 steps)
1. Extract thematic keywords from narrative teams (don't invent yourself)
2. Develop mood boards around emotional pillars (not just visual refs)
3. Break design into **shape, color, and texture** systems separately
4. Ground abstract elements in **existing IP** (character assets, not just style refs)
5. Create comprehensive visual guidelines for **cross-platform consistency**

## Implications for capability map
- "Thematic keyword extraction from narrative" is an actionable skill
- "Shape/color/texture system separation" is testable
- "IP grounding via existing asset patterns" is a critique-checkable principle
- "Cross-platform guideline production" is a deliverable
