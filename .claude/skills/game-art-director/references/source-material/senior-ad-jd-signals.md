## Synthesized responsibilities (recurring across listings)

1. **Creative leadership**: "guide the vision and manage the art team" — vision is not a slide deck, it's a daily decision standard
2. **Team-facing quality bar**: "ensure team's deliverables meet the highest standards of artistic quality"
3. **Mission/direction/cohesion** (Riot Associate AD): three explicit deliverables — mission alignment + direction clarity + cohesion enforcement
4. **Sub-discipline ownership** at senior level (e.g., "lead the Character Art efforts on a project") — AD is not generalist; senior AD owns one craft area deeply
5. **Cross-discipline craft fluency** preferred — animation, VFX, lighting, comp must be readable
6. **Production realism integration** (Technical AD): "content creation ecosystem is efficient, scalable and built to support both creative excellence and production realities"

## Senior AD bar (synthesized — most JDs converge on these)

- 8-12+ years industry experience in art / illustration / concept
- Shipped at least 1 AAA title or 2 acclaimed indie titles at AD or senior IC level
- Portfolio demonstrating range AND a clear authorial voice (not generic skill demonstration)
- Articulate written + verbal critique — must be able to give actionable, non-defensive feedback that team can execute
- Ability to write / maintain **style guide artifacts** (Riot specifically prizes this)
- "Curiosity about [IP / world / theme]" — JDs explicitly call this out, suggesting most candidates fail on this

## Implications for 0→1 阶段 capability map

- 视觉 pillar / theme keyword 制定能力 = Tier-1 always loaded
- Critique 给反馈的写作能力 = Tier-1（不是"画得好"）
- Style guide 写作能力 = Tier-1
- 跨工种 readability（VFX / animation / lighting） = Tier-2，按任务调
- 工业流水线（pipeline 决策、外包标准、批改 review）= Tier-2，0→1 阶段不是首要
- IP / world / theme 的好奇心和深读能力 = anchor 级，不只是 capability
