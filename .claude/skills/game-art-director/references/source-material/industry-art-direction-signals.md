## Industry framework (from Polydin article — widely-repeated baseline)

### Art Director 5 核心职责
1. **Defining visual style** — 美学、色彩 scheme、设计原则
2. **Team leadership** — 引导艺术家，确保方向遵从
3. **Cross-disciplinary collaboration** — 与 game design / programming / audio 协同
4. **Quality control** — review assets against established standards
5. **Problem solving** — 在约束内解决视觉与技术挑战

### 决策流程（5 step）
1. Develop strong vision aligned with narrative + mechanics + audience
2. Create style guides as reference documents
3. Foster cross-departmental collaboration
4. Provide clear, constructive feedback regularly
5. Balance creative ambition against technical/budgetary limits

### 行业共识 quote
- "Immersion depends on consistency."
- "Players don't just remember what they did in a game; they remember how the game made them feel."
- "Visual cues can help guide players through levels, indicate interactive objects, and convey important information."

## 评价维度（Wayline article — 反 mediocre 立场）

### 核心 thesis
> "Art direction is not merely cosmetic; it is a fundamental aspect of the player experience."

### 失败模式（直接成为 anti-patterns 输入）
- **Homogenization risk**: "widespread pixel-art adoption creates indistinguishable games that blend into the background"
- **Gameplay undermining**: "a mediocre art style can actively detract from the gameplay experience"
- **Generic styles fail**: "lack originality and fail to capture the imagination of players"

### 评价 4 维
- Visual complexity (moderately complex 最优)
- Color palette coherence (harmony enhances appeal)
- Visual distinctiveness (memorability factor)
- Narrative-visual alignment (emotional resonance)

### Positive 例
- **Hollow Knight** — hand-drawn, intricate, narrative-gameplay 整合
- **Supergiant Games** (Bastion / Transistor / Hades) — signature style as brand asset

### Negative 例
- 泛 pixel-art indie 集群（未点名）

## 关于 0→1 定调期的隐含约束（综合信号）

从 Spirit Blossom + Polydin + Wayline 综合：

1. **Style guide 必须早做**——不是"等定下来再写"，而是边定边写。否则团队会自由发挥到不可收回
2. **关键词驱动 > image 驱动**——Spirit Blossom 的 6 个 keyword 是决策锚点；纯 image board 容易陷入"reference 拼贴"
3. **IP grounding 是 0→1 阶段的杠杆**——不要 from-scratch，从现有资产里抽 pattern
4. **Shape / color / texture 三轴分别建立**——不要混在一起讨论
5. **"独特性" 不是 luxury，是 commercial requirement**——Wayline 反复强调
