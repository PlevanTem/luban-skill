## 算法 / 机制关键事实

- **Employee content 8x company page 的 engagement** — founder/CEO/VP 个人账号发优于 brand page
- **Carousel 278% > video > 静态 post** — format hierarchy 明确
- **Video 5x > 静态** — 但 carousel 仍是顶
- **Thought leadership 2-3x > 标准 brand post** — content type ranking
- Top quartile 用 30% 更多 audience filters 给 ad targeting
- Algorithm reward **expertise + engagement quality**，不只看 frequency
- Meaningful comments extends reach（algorithm 视 comment quality）

## 内容形态层级（按 engagement 强→弱）

1. Founder/CEO personal carousel + framework post
2. Employee personal video / 案例分享
3. Thought leadership post（带具体观点 + 经验）
4. Company page carousel
5. Company page video
6. Company page 静态 post（最弱）
